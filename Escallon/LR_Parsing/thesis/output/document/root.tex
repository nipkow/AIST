\vspace*{2cm}
\begin{center}
    {\Large \bf Abstract}
\end{center}
\vspace{1cm}

This thesis presents a formal verification of $LR(0)$ parsing theory as presented by Wilhelm et
al.~\cite{Wilhelm}, as well as a proof that the $LR(0)$ parser accepts exactly the language of the
context-free grammar from which it is constructed.
