\title{Formal Verification of LR(0) Parsing Theory}
\author{Felipe Escall\'{o}n}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}

This paper presents a formal verification of the LR(0) parsing algorithms and theory as specified by 
Wilhelm et al.~\cite{Wilhelm}, as well as a proof that the language accepted by the canonical LR(0) 
parser is exactly that of its underlying context-free grammar. 

\end{abstract}
